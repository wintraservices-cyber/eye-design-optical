Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead means less waiting, especially on weekends.

How long does an eye check take?

Most check-ups take about 20 to 30 minutes. Choosing frames takes as long as you like.

Where did the Robinsons Novaliches branch go?

We've moved to 19B Pontiac St., West Fairview, near Panko Bakery and Cafe. Same team, new home.

Can I bring my old prescription?

Yes. Bring your old glasses or prescription so we can compare and see what changed.

Do you check children's eyes?

Yes. Kids often don't say they can't see the board, so a check before school starts is a good idea.
