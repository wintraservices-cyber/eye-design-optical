Eye care around Fairview

Patients searching for an eye check near Fairview will mostly find the mall chains first.

Mall optical chains

EO Executive Optical and Optiview at Fairview Terraces, and Sarabia Optical at SM City Fairview. Big brands with mall foot traffic, and EO branches elsewhere in Metro Manila show hundreds of Google reviews and online booking buttons on clinic directories.

Novagen Eye Center, SM City Fairview

An ophthalmology and eye surgery center. Its doctors' clinic schedules can be booked through SeriousMD. The place for surgery and eye disease, not a quick check and new frames.

Eye Design Optical Clinic

A neighborhood clinic on Pontiac St.: no mall parking, no queue behind shoppers, and the same optometrist every visit. The service is already the advantage. What's missing is being as easy to find and book as the chains.

The pitch in one line: the chains win on being findable. Eye Design can win on being close, personal, and just as easy to book, starting with the 3,900 people who already follow the page.
